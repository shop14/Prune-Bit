1 Getting Started

Welcome to PruneBit! This guide will help you get started with managing your cryptocurrency securely.

New to Crypto?

If you're new to cryptocurrency, we recommend reading our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy first.

What You'll Need

  • A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
  • A secure internet connection
  • A strong password (minimum 8 characters recommended)

First Steps

  1. Choose whether to create a new wallet or import an existing one
  2. Set a strong, unique password
  3. Write down your seed phrase and store it securely
  4. Start managing your cryptocurrency!

2 Creating a New Wallet

Creating a new wallet generates a unique BIP39/BIP32 mnemonic phrase that gives you full control over your funds.

1

Navigate to Create Wallet

Click "Create New Wallet" on the homepage or go to setup.html.

2

Choose Your Coin

Select the cryptocurrency you want to create a wallet for.

3

Set a Strong Password

Create a password that you'll use to access your wallet.

4

Save Your Seed Phrase

You'll receive a 12 or 24-word seed phrase. Write this down and store it safely.

Important

Never share your seed phrase with anyone. Anyone with access to your seed phrase can control your wallet.

3 Accessing Your Wallet

To access your existing wallet, you'll need your password.

Steps to Access

  1. Click "Access Wallet" on the homepage or go to import.html
  2. Enter your wallet password
  3. Click "Unlock Wallet"
  4. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

Session Management

Your session will remain active for 15 minutes of inactivity. After that, you'll need to log in again.

4 Importing an Existing Wallet

You can import a wallet using a mnemonic phrase or private key from another wallet.

Mnemonic Phrase

Import using your 12 or 24-word seed phrase from another wallet.

  1. Go to import.html
  2. Select "Mnemonic Phrase"
  3. Enter your seed phrase
  4. Set a new password

Private Key

Import using a private key (WIF format for BTC/BTCT, hex for ETH).

  1. Go to import.html
  2. Select "Private Key"
  3. Enter your private key
  4. Set a new password

5 Sending Cryptocurrency

Send cryptocurrency to any valid address with custom fee control.

How to Send

  1. Access your wallet and go to the "Send" page
  2. Select the cryptocurrency you want to send
  3. Enter the recipient's address
  4. Enter the amount to send
  5. Choose your fee (Low, Medium, High, or Custom)
  6. Review the transaction details
  7. Click "Send Transaction"

Fee Guide

Fee LevelConfirmation TimeUse Case
Low~30 minutesNon-urgent transactions
Medium~10 minutesStandard transactions
High~5 minutesUrgent transactions

6 Receiving Cryptocurrency

Receive cryptocurrency by sharing your wallet address with the sender.

How to Receive

  1. Access your wallet and go to the "Receive" page
  2. Select the cryptocurrency you want to receive
  3. Copy your wallet address or show the QR code
  4. Share the address with the sender

Tip

Always verify that the sender is sending the correct cryptocurrency to the correct address. Cryptocurrency transactions are irreversible.

7 Security Best Practices

Follow these guidelines to keep your cryptocurrency safe.

Do

  • Write down your seed phrase
  • Store seed phrase offline
  • Use a strong, unique password
  • Verify addresses before sending
  • Keep your software updated

Don't

  • Share your seed phrase
  • Store seed phrase digitally
  • Reuse passwords
  • Click suspicious links
  • Enter seed phrase on websites

9 Address Check

Verify and validate cryptocurrency addresses before sending funds to ensure they are correct and supported.

1

Go to Address Check

Navigate to the "Address Check" page from the dashboard or main menu.

2

Enter the Address

Paste or type the cryptocurrency address you want to verify. The tool supports Bitcoin, Litecoin, Ethereum, XRP, and more.

3

Review Results

The tool will validate the address format, detect the network type, and confirm whether it's a valid address for the specified cryptocurrency.

Why Use Address Check?

Sending cryptocurrency to an invalid or incorrect address can result in permanent loss of funds. Always verify the address before initiating a transaction.

11 Exchange Crypto

Exchange one cryptocurrency for another directly through PruneBit. Our exchange feature supports multiple coin pairs with competitive rates.

1

Select Exchange Pair

Choose the cryptocurrency you want to exchange from and the one you want to receive. Supported pairs include BTC, LTC, DGB, ETH, XRP, and more.

2

Enter Amount

Enter the amount you want to exchange. The estimated receiving amount will be calculated automatically based on current rates.

3

Provide Receiving Address

Enter the destination address where you want to receive the exchanged cryptocurrency.

4

Confirm and Exchange

Review the exchange details including rate, fees, and estimated time, then confirm to initiate the exchange.

Exchange rates are estimated and may change slightly before confirmation. The final rate is locked once you confirm the exchange.

12 Troubleshooting

If you forgot your password but have your seed phrase, you can import your wallet using the seed phrase and set a new password. Go to import.html and select "Mnemonic Phrase".

Note: Without your password AND seed phrase, your wallet cannot be recovered.

Transaction confirmation times depend on network congestion and the fee you paid. If your transaction is taking too long:

  1. Check the transaction status on a blockchain explorer
  2. If using Bitcoin, you can use RBF (Replace-By-Fee) to speed it up
  3. For future transactions, consider using a higher fee

If you have your password, you can still access your wallet. However, if you lose both your password and seed phrase, your wallet cannot be recovered. This is why it's crucial to back up your seed phrase in a secure location.

Yes, PruneBit is free to use. We don't charge any fees for wallet creation, management, or transactions. However, blockchain network fees (miner fees) still apply when sending transactions.

PruneBit uses industry-standard security measures:

  • AES-256-GCM encryption for seed phrases
  • Bcrypt hashing (work factor 12) for passwords
  • BIP39/BIP32 standard for wallet generation
  • No storage of private keys on our servers

Your funds are as secure as your seed phrase and password.

AML Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Compliance Commitment

PruneBit is committed to preventing money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion, fraud, and other illicit activity through risk-based controls and responsible service use.

1. Purpose

This Anti-Money Laundering Policy describes the controls, expectations, and user responsibilities that apply when using PruneBit.

The policy is intended to support legal and compliant use of cryptocurrency wallet services and to help reduce misuse of the platform.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all users, wallets, transactions, addresses, and related activity associated with PruneBit.

It covers wallet creation, importing, sending, receiving, transaction history review, and any administrative review of wallet activity.

3. Prohibited Activity

Users may not use PruneBit for activities involving:

  • Money laundering or concealment of illicit funds
  • Terrorist financing or sanctions evasion
  • Fraud, scams, phishing, or social engineering
  • Stolen funds or funds linked to unauthorized access
  • Illegal goods or services
  • Market manipulation or other unlawful financial activity

4. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for using PruneBit lawfully and for understanding the source and destination of funds associated with their wallet activity.

  • Do not send, receive, or manage funds connected to illegal activity.
  • Verify recipient addresses before submitting transactions.
  • Keep your password, seed phrase, and recovery information secure.
  • Do not share account credentials, wallet access, or encrypted wallet data with unauthorized persons.
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations in your jurisdiction.

5. Monitoring and Review

PruneBit may review wallet activity, balances, incidents, and system records to detect suspicious, unlawful, or abusive activity.

Administrative review tools are intended to help identify wallet activity and system records without changing the normal user wallet workflow.

6. Suspicious Activity

PruneBit may take appropriate action when activity appears suspicious, unlawful, or inconsistent with this policy.

Possible actions include account review, service restrictions, incident logging, support escalation, or reporting to competent authorities where required by law.

7. Sanctions and Restricted Parties

Users must not use PruneBit in violation of applicable economic sanctions, export controls, or restricted-party requirements.

PruneBit may restrict access or activity when required to comply with applicable sanctions or legal obligations.

8. Record Keeping

PruneBit may retain records related to wallet identifiers, encrypted wallet data, session activity, balances, transactions, incidents, and administrative review events as permitted by law and as described in the Privacy Policy.

Users should keep their own records of transactions, addresses, and wallet backups for tax, compliance, and personal accounting purposes.

9. Cooperation

Users agree to cooperate with reasonable compliance, security, and support requests related to lawful use of the Service.

Failure to cooperate with lawful requests may result in restricted access, account review, or termination of service where permitted.

10. Changes to This Policy

PruneBit may update this AML Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page, and users should review this policy periodically for changes.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this AML Policy or need to report suspicious activity, please contact PruneBit support through the support page.

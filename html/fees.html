All About Fees

A complete overview of fees for using PruneBit wallet services.

Deposit Fees

PruneBit does not charge any deposit fees. Receiving crypto into your wallet is always free. You only pay the standard network fee for the transaction to be confirmed on the blockchain.

Exchange Fees

When exchanging one cryptocurrency for another, a service fee applies in addition to the network fee for each side of the exchange.

Frequently Asked Questions

To reduce fees, you can:
  • Send during off-peak hours when the network is less congested.
  • Use cryptocurrencies with lower network fees like Litecoin, Dogecoin, or Stellar.
  • Consolidate small UTXOs (Bitcoin) when fees are low to avoid high fees later.
  • For same-chain exchanges, fees are lower than cross-chain exchanges.

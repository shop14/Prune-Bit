Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you keep my keys or seed phrase?
A: No. The server stores only a bcrypt PIN hash and public addresses. Don't take our word for it — read the code: classes/Wallet.php.

Q: Can you recover my wallet if I lose my seed phrase?
A: No — and neither can anyone else. This is what non-custodial means. Keep your seed phrase safe.

Q: Is the app open source?
A: Yes, under the MIT license.

Custody Model

Non-Custodial Wallet

PruneBit never holds, pools, or controls user funds. Seed phrases are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and only decrypted in your browser session with your PIN. Because user assets are never custodied, no reserve backing is required or claimed.

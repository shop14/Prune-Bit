Security and Standards

The wallet keeps the original local-first flow: create, import, unlock, and manage sessions without email-based accounts. Seed phrases are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Seed wallets use standard HD paths, including Ethereum path m/44'/60'/0'/0/0.

Wallet sessions are token-based and tied to the wallet ID, not an email account. Your keys stay on your machine.
